Where elevated burden persisted

We classified each tract against one historical benchmark: the 75th percentile of 2015–2019 tract rates, approximately 13.52 events per 10,000 population per year

Transition counts will appear after the data load.

“Previously elevated” means below this benchmark in the recent period, not that violence disappeared. With a fixed benchmark and widespread declines, we do not expect many newly elevated tracts

Methods, sources, and aggregate data

2020 Census tracts; pooled three-year counts divided by fixed population × 3, multiplied by 10,000. Officer-involved shootings were excluded. Event location =/= residence. Zero or missing populations produce unavailable rates

Sources: Philadelphia shooting-victim data (extract 2026-05-21); US Census Bureau 2020 geography and 2020–2024 ACS five-year total population
